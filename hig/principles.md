# Design principles

Source: [Apple HIG — Design principles](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/design-principles).
These are tools for weighing priorities, not a single recipe.

Traceway is a solo route planner. It draws a loop on a map and exports
a course file. The voice is calm and precise.

## Purpose

The useful act is the loop, then the file. A screen that does not help
someone draw a route or take that course to a watch or GPS does not
earn its chrome.

Keep the planner on sport, length, hills, surface, a via, and loop or
out-and-back. Study other route tools without copying their clubs,
heat, or marks. What sets Traceway apart is a quiet loop and a file
you can leave with.

## Agency

Get to the map. The header and the bar stay present and small. Do not
lock a person into a wizard to draw one loop.

Filters redraw. A poor loop can be drawn again. Leaving a page does
not spend the course they already exported.

A guest who opens `/dashboard` goes to log in and comes back to the
planner. The detour is the door, not a new product.

## Responsibility

Say what this is from the first line: it draws a route and offers a
course file. It does not watch a ride, score an effort, or gather a
club.

Ask only for the account the planner needs. Permission and error copy
state the reason next to the control. Do not imply a network of riders.

## Familiarity

One header, one type scale, one set of colors, on the site and in the
planner. A loop is a loop. GPX and FIT are files, named as files.

Once a control behaves a way, keep it. The filled action is the next
physical step: open the planner, draw the route, or send the form.
Status is a word, not a color trick.

## Flexibility

Keyboard and pointer both reach every control. Text can grow. The page
can narrow. The map is the picture of the route, not the only record
of what happened: distance, status, and errors stay in text.

Do not build a Mac-only gesture or a hover-only action. The site is
used in a browser, at a desk and on a phone.

## Simplicity

Include the route and the file. Simplicity is not an empty page. Sport,
length, elevation, surface, via, and the loop switch stay in one bar.
Everything else falls away.

Use the shortest physical wording. Hierarchy is size, weight, and
color, in that order of reading: page, title, body, meta, then the
one filled action.

## Craft

Be deliberate about type, motion, wording, and the space scale. Both
stylesheets carry the same tokens. Corners share a center. Weights stay
at 400, 500, and 600. Nothing thin, nothing glowing, nothing borrowed
from another brand’s orange.

Prototype against the four surfaces. A screen that only looks right in
one app is not done.

## Delight

The feeling to leave is calm and precise: a clean loop, then a course
file that leaves with you. That is the moment. It does not need a
second animation.

Delight is not decoration. People are trying to draw a route and go.
Freedom to redraw, a familiar header, and a file they can hold are the
pleasure. Extra shadow, extra tracking, and extra motion are not.
