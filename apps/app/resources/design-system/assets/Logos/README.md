The Traceway mark. Single-ink files, because an `<img>` cannot inherit colour: `traceway-mark-ink.svg` strokes in ink (#071f20) for Paper grounds, `traceway-mark-paper.svg` in paper (#f7f5ec) for Ink grounds. Both keep the dot in `marker-dot` (#97cb46). `traceway-app-icon.svg` is the favicon: the ink mark on a paper tile with a 7px corner on a 32 grid. In React, use `AppLogoIcon`, which strokes in `currentColor`.
