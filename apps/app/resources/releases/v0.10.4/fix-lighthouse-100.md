section: Fixes
---
- **Pages open faster.** The log in and sign up pages, the status page, and a shared route show at once, even on a slow phone connection.
- **Text stays put.** Words no longer jump when the letters finish loading.
- **Your location only when you ask.** The planner no longer asks for your location as it opens. Tap the locate button on the map to start where you are. If you allowed it before, the map still finds you.
- **Easier to read on a shared route.** The full length under the player is no longer pale grey on white.
