## Multisport

- A **Multisport** tab plans a brick, triathlon, duathlon, or aquabike as one session.
  - An optional swim, drawn on the water.
  - One to four land legs, each with its own sport, length, surface, hills, and colour.
- Official race distances, from a super sprint triathlon to a full Ironman, and the standard duathlon and aquabike distances.
- Legs can all loop from the transition, or chain so each one ends where the next begins.
- Each land leg downloads as its own Garmin course, and the GPX file holds every leg.
- Every route has a player. Slide it, or press play, and a pin follows the line.

## On your Garmin

- **Send to device** downloads the course and shows how to get it onto a Garmin, through Garmin Connect or over USB.
- **The Traceway app for Connect IQ** lists your saved routes on the watch or Edge. Pair it once with a code under **Settings → Devices**. Pick a route and it starts in the device's own navigation.

## Plan from your AI

- Ask ChatGPT, Claude, or another AI tool for "a 40 km gravel loop from Westerlo" or "a sprint triathlon from the lake". The route comes from the map, never from the AI, and it is saved to your library.
- Every app asks you on a Traceway page first. **Settings → Your data** lists connected apps, and one tap disconnects one.

## Faster

- Long routes draw several times faster, and a full Ironman plan takes about 12 seconds instead of 45. Rides over 150 km now draw.

## Easier to use

- The map works with the keyboard: the arrow keys move it, and Enter places a point.
