section: New
---
- **Avoid a road.** Tap a road on your loop and choose Avoid this road, or use Avoid a road in the panel. Traceway skips that stretch on every plan from then on, until you remove it.
- **Your avoided roads stay yours.** They are shown on the map with a red dashed line, listed in the panel and under Your data, and only your own plans skip them.
- **Says when there is no way around.** If a route still has to use a road you avoid, the panel tells you.
