section: New
---
- **Find a place by name.** Type a town, a street, or an address in the search bar on the map, and press Enter to look everywhere.
- **Typos are forgiven.** While you type, the bar suggests streets and towns near you, even with a letter wrong.
- **A tap asks first.** Tapping the map shows a small card, such as Start here or Go via here, so a slip or a pan never moves your pin.
- **Pins land on a road or path.** Your start, a via, a point, or a finish always sits on a way you can ride or run, never in a field.
- **Drag your pins.** Move the start, the via, and the points of a built route by dragging them.
- **Undo.** Moved, added, or removed a pin by mistake? Press Undo.
- **Places keep their own names.** Towns and countries read the way people there write them, like Den Haag or Antwerpen.
