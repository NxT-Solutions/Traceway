## Error pages

- Every error page is now a Traceway page. A runner, a hiker, or a cyclist keeps moving along a night trail, and the error code hangs on a race bib.
  - "Off the map" when a page is missing.
  - "We hit a pothole" when something breaks on our side.
  - "Easy on the pace" when you click too fast.
  - "At the aid station" during upkeep.
- Each page says in one plain line what happened, with a button back to the start, the planner, or a retry.
