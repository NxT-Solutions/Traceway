## Stops that load

- **Water, toilets, and cafés load again, and fast.** The public server they came from was overloaded and often gave up. Traceway now reads them from the same map tiles the planner draws: about two seconds for a 200 km route.

## Your privacy, in writing

- **New pages:** a privacy policy, terms of use, cookies, and a legal notice, linked at the bottom of the site.
- **Sign-up and every account email** link the terms and the privacy policy.
- **Your pins stay yours.** When you drop a point, Traceway looks up its place name itself, for a point rounded to about 100 metres. Your browser no longer sends the exact spot to another company.

## Limits

- **A GPX file can be up to 300 km**, the longest loop the planner draws. A longer one is refused, and the message says how long it is.

## Behind the scenes

- The server deletes what it no longer needs: unsaved routes after two hours, place names after 30 days, and an email that could not be sent after a day.
