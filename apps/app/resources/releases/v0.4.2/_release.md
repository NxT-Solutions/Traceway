title: Clearer route buttons, and a safer home for your routes
summary: Route buttons only look clickable when they are, and your routes are kept safe off-site.

---

## Planner

- **Export GPX**, **Send to device**, and **Save to profile** look greyed out until you draw a route. Before, they looked ready at 0.0 km but did nothing.

## Your routes, kept safe

- Your account and routes are copied to off-site storage every few seconds, so a server failure no longer means lost routes.
