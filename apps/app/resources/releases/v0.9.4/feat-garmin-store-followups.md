section: Devices
---
- **Sport icons fit on the Edge 1030 and 1040.** The bike and shoe icons next to each route are no longer cut off at the edge of the screen.
- **The whole line on a round watch.** On a Forerunner or fenix, the highlighted route shows its full distance and climb. From 10 km it shows whole kilometres, so the climb fits.
- **A sharper Traceway logo.** On Edge bike computers the logo's letters were drawn bold and jagged. They are smooth now.
- **Labels and icons in the middle.** Ride it and Save are centred in their buttons, and the sport icons sit in the middle of each route on watches and the newer Edges.
- **The route fills the map.** On smaller Edges, such as the 830 and 840, and the tall 850, the route was drawn small on the map. It now fills the space above the buttons.
