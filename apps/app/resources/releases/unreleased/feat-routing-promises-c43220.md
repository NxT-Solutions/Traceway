section: Fixes
---
- **Each sport starts on its own ground.** Pick trail run, hike, or mountain bike and Surface now changes to Dirt. Walk and gravel change to Any, and run and road stay on Paved. You can still change it under Surface.
- **Quiet first works on foot.** A run, walk, or hike now takes footpaths, tracks, and quiet streets before roads where it can. In a town, about half of a run is now off the road, up from a third.
- **Dirt on foot finds tracks and footpaths.** Before, Dirt could lead a run or a hike along town streets. Now it looks for footpaths and farm and forest tracks first.
- **Trail runs and hikes can use mountain paths.** Steeper, rougher paths that were left out before can now be part of the route.
