section: Fixes
---
- **Flat means flatter.** With Flat picked, the planner now weighs how much each loop climbs and offers the flattest one near your length. Hilly does the opposite.
- **Loops come closer to your length.** When a loop comes out too short or too long, the planner tries again in that direction on a bigger or smaller circle. A long ride is held closer: within about 1.5 km on a 10 km run, and 5 km on a 100 km ride.
- **Always three loops.** You get three loops to pick from, closest to your length first, also when a via point is set.
- **No more small knots.** A loop no longer rides out a side road, circles a block, and comes back the same way.
- **A tidier sport list.** Choose a sport no longer has a search box: with seven sports, the list is quicker to read.
- **Remove stays put.** Letting an avoided road back in no longer makes the panel jump while the loop draws again, so a quick second click cannot land on Save by mistake.
