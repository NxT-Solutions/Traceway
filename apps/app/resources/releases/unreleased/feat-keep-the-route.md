---
section: New
---
- **Move a point, keep your route.** In Build, a point you drag, add, or remove only changes the roads on either side of it. The rest of the route stays as you drew it.
- **Bend a loop through a place.** Go via here now bends the loop you have, instead of drawing three new ones. If the loop gets much longer, Traceway says so and offers New loops through here.
- **Avoiding a road changes only that part.** When you avoid a road your route uses, only the stretch that used it is planned again.
- **Undo goes further back.** Undo now takes back several changes, one at a time, and Redo puts them back. Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Shift+Z work too, or Cmd on a Mac.
