---
section: Devices
---
- **Pick your device.** Send to device now asks which device you have, and shows the file it reads and a few short steps for Garmin, Wahoo, Hammerhead Karoo, Bryton, COROS, Suunto, Polar, Amazfit, and Apple Watch.
- **One tap to Wahoo.** Connect your Wahoo account once under Settings, then Devices. A route goes to your ELEMNT or ACE in one tap, and sending it again updates it.
- **Send to Ride with GPS.** Connect Ride with GPS once and a route lands in your library there. Pin it, and your Karoo or Bryton picks it up.
- **GPX for every leg.** Each land leg of a multisport plan now downloads as GPX too, for watches that do not read FIT.
