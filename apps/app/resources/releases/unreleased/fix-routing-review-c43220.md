section: Fixes
---
- **Surface and Roads say what they do on foot.** On a run, trail run, walk, or hike, Quiet first puts walkways and pavements first, and Dirt puts tracks and walkways first. A busy road can still be part of the route.
- **Any is its own choice on foot.** Any now leans a little towards tracks, between Paved and Dirt. Before, it planned the same as one of them.
- **Clearer words for bikes.** Dirt says it uses fewer roads and allows rough ground, and Any says that smooth roads still win.
- **Points land where a road bike can go.** On Paved, a road ride's start, via, or Build point no longer lands on a track or a way the map marks as unpaved. If no paved road is near, the planner says so. In a multisport plan, the end of a leg lands on a way that leg's sport can use.
- **Hills say what they do.** Any steers round steep climbs. Hilly adds no extra push round climbs, and a steep one still counts as slow.
