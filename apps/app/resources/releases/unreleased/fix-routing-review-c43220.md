section: Fixes
---
- **Surface and Roads say what they do on foot.** On a run, walk, or hike, Quiet first puts footpaths first, and Dirt puts tracks and footpaths first. A busy road can still be part of the route.
- **Any is its own choice on foot.** Any now leans a little towards tracks, between Paved and Dirt. Before, it planned the same as one of them.
- **Switching sports sets a suitable surface.** A plan you already have keeps its own, and you can always change it under Surface.
- **Points land where the route can go.** On Paved, a road ride's start or via no longer lands on a gravel track. In a multisport plan, the end of a leg lands on a way that leg's sport can use.
- **Hills say what they do.** Any steers round steep climbs a little, and Hilly stops steering round them.
