section: Fixes
---
- **Surface and Roads say what they do on foot.** On a run, trail run, walk, or hike, Quiet first puts walkways first, and Dirt puts tracks and walkways first. A busy road can still be part of the route.
- **Any is its own choice on foot.** Any now leans a little towards tracks, between Paved and Dirt. Before, it planned the same as one of them.
- **Clearer words for bikes.** Dirt says it uses fewer roads and allows rough ground, and Any that a road bike still leans to smooth roads. Any road says roads count for more, and that on Dirt it changes nothing.
- **Sports say only what they do.** Trail run, hike, walk, run, mountain bike, and gravel no longer promise hills, rough track, or the same loop as a run.
- **Points are found for your sport and surface.** When no road is drawn near the spot, the start or via is found on a way your sport and surface can use. For a road ride on Paved with no such road near, the planner says so.
- **Each leg's end is found for its own sport.** In a multisport plan, when no road is drawn near, the end of a leg is found for that leg's sport and surface, not the leg you are editing.
- **Hills say what they do.** Any steers round steep climbs. Hilly adds no extra push round climbs, and a steep one still counts as slow.
