---
section: Devices
---
- **See what is on your device.** In the Traceway app on your Garmin, a route you saved to Courses has a green icon in the list.
- **Remove a route from your device.** Open a saved route and choose Remove. The device asks first, then takes it out of Courses. You can save it again at any time.
- **Neater on every Garmin.** The sport icon sits in the middle of each route in the list, and is no longer cut off on the Edge 530, 830, and Explore. The two buttons under a route are the same size, with their words in the middle.
