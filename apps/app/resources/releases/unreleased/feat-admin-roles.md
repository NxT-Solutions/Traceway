section: Privacy
---
- **A clearer privacy policy.** It now says that Traceway notes when you last used it, and keeps daily totals of what is used that never say who.
- **Who can help with your account.** The policy now says what the people who run Traceway can see, and what they can do when you ask them for help.
- **A record of every change.** Anything the people who run Traceway do to your account is written down for a year, and it is in your copy of your data.
- **Your data copy has a little more.** The copy of your data now shows when you last used Traceway and your role.
