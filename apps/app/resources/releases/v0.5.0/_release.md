## The ride, before you go

- **Climbs, ground, turns, and stops** for every route: generated, built, opened from a GPX, each multisport leg, saved routes, and shared links.
  - A climb chart, with a steepness view on the map.
  - The surface split: paved, gravel, and dirt.
  - The turns, and the water, toilets, and cafés on the way. Tap one and the map takes you there.
- **Bring a GPX file.** Keep it as it is, ride it the other way round, or start it from your own door. It keeps going forward instead of doubling back.

## On the watch

- **A warning before each turn**, and before water, a toilet, or a café.
- **The climbs ahead.** Heights and total climbing are in the route file, so ClimbPro on a Garmin works.
- Other watches and bike computers get the heights and the stops in the GPX file.

## Shared links

- **A clean picture of the route on the map**, with a clear title, wherever you paste the link. Shared links stay out of search engines.
- **The share page shows the whole ride:** the climb, the steepness, the stops, the turns, and how it was planned.

## Plan by asking your AI

- **Settings → AI assistants** explains how it works and how to connect it.
- **In Claude, the route shows up as a map you can move and zoom**, right in the chat, saved to your library and ready for your watch.

## Behind the scenes

- Routes you saved before this update got their climb, turns, and stops on their own.
- Easier to use with a keyboard or a screen reader: clearer contrast, and buttons that say what they do.
