# Space and radius

Spacing is a 4px base with the half steps the planner really uses. Controls are 10px (`radius-lg`); options inside them sit 4px in at 7px, so the corners stay concentric. Surfaces are flat, divided by hairlines; only what floats over the map gets `shadow-panel`.
