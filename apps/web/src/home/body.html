How it works

Three moves. Then you go.

No fiddling with the map. Say where, how far, and which sport. Traceway finds the route.

01

Drop a pin

Your driveway, the trailhead, the park. The loop starts and ends right there, on foot or on a bike.

02

Name the distance and the sport

40 km on the road. 12 km on foot. A ride and a run read the map differently, so the same pin gives a different loop.

03

Take it with you

Put it on your Garmin, COROS, Suunto, Wahoo, or Hammerhead. It shows you the way by itself. The phone can stay at home.

No feed.No club.No public routes.

Traceway is a solo planner. Your pin, your loop and your Sunday morning stay yours. Nobody sees where you start unless you share a link, nobody ranks your day, and your routes are never listed for anyone else.

There is nothing to scroll and nobody to keep up with. You get a line that is worth the day, and the quiet to take it. The map is OpenStreetMap. Choosing the loop that fits your day is our job.

Questions

Before you drop the pin.

How do I plan a loop that starts and ends at my house?

Drop the pin on your house, pick a sport and a distance, and Traceway plans a loop that comes home another way where the roads allow. Or type your address into the search. You can ask for 5 to 200 km.

Can it make a running loop or a circular walk for me?

Yes. Pick run, trail run, walk, or hike, give a distance, and Traceway plans a loop from your pin.

Will the loop be as long as I asked?

Usually close. Traceway tries a few loops near your distance and picks the best, so asking for 40 km usually gives you about 40 km. Where roads are few, it can come out longer or shorter, and the planner shows the real length.

Can it find me a flat route?

Yes. Under Hills, pick Flat, and the loop avoids climbing where the roads allow. Pick Hilly instead to let it take the hills rather than go round them. The planner shows how many metres you go up and down, and can colour the steep parts on the map.

Can I keep to paved roads, or find gravel?

Yes, on a bike. Under Surface, pick Paved to favour tarmac, or Dirt to use fewer roads and allow unpaved tracks. The planner then shows how many kilometres are paved, gravel, or dirt.

Can it keep me off busy main roads?

On a bike, yes, as far as the roads allow. Leave Roads on Quiet first, as it starts, and the loop leans on cycleways, paths, and small roads. On foot, the loop does not steer away from busy roads.

Can I make the loop pass a particular place?

Add a via point. The loop bends through it and still brings you home, and tries not to use the same road twice.

Will it show where to find water, a toilet, or a café on the way?

Yes. The planner marks water, toilets, and cafés just off your route, and lists how far along the route each one is. On a Garmin, your watch or bike computer warns you before you reach them.

Can I follow the route without my phone?

Yes. Load the route once, and your watch or bike computer shows you the way by itself. You need no phone and no mobile signal on the way.

Which watches and bike computers work?

Garmin, COROS, Suunto, Wahoo, Hammerhead, and most others that can load a route. On a Garmin you also get a warning before turns, and before water, a toilet, or a café.

How do I send a route to my Garmin and follow it?

On most Edge bike computers, the Fenix 7, and the Forerunner 255, 265, 955, 965, and 970, pair the Traceway app once with a code, and your latest saved routes load over your phone. Pick one and press Ride it, Run it, Walk it, or Hike it, and the device shows you the way. On other Garmins that can follow a course, press Send to device in the planner and add the route in Garmin Connect.

How do I send a route to a Wahoo, COROS, Suunto, or another brand?

Press Send to device and pick your brand. The planner gives you the right file and the steps for Wahoo, Hammerhead, COROS, Suunto, Bryton, Polar, and others. You can also connect a Wahoo or Ride with GPS account once under Settings, then Devices, and send each route there in one tap.

Can I put a GPX file someone gave me on my watch or bike computer?

Yes. Open the GPX file in the planner, for a route up to 300 km, and keep it as it is, turn it round, or start it from your own door. Save it to find it in the Traceway app on your Garmin, or press Send to device for any other watch or bike computer.

Can I share a route with a friend?

Yes. Open the route in Saved routes, press Copy link, and send it. Your friend can see it on a map and download it for their own watch or bike computer, without an account.

Can I follow the route before I go?

Yes. Press play on the map and a pin rides the whole route for you.

Can I plan a triathlon or a brick?

Yes. Pick a brick, a triathlon, a duathlon, or an aquabike, then one of its race distances, and the legs fill in. Or set each leg yourself.

Does Traceway route the swim?

No, there are no roads on the water. You draw the swim on the map yourself, and the planner shows how far you have drawn.

Can friends meet me at a handover?

Yes. Put each handover on the map and give each leg its distance. Drag a handover to move it.

Can ChatGPT or Claude plan a route and make a GPX file?

Yes, once you connect Traceway (ChatGPT needs a paid plan for that). Add traceway.online/mcp as a connector, sign in once, then ask for "a 40 km gravel loop from Westerlo". Traceway, not the AI, plans it on real roads and paths and saves it to your library, with a Garmin file and a GPX file for your watch.

Will it work where I live, or on holiday?

Yes. The pin goes anywhere with roads and paths on OpenStreetMap, from your own door to a hotel in a town you do not know. The loops on this page start in Westerlo, Belgium, where Traceway is built.

Do I need an account?

Yes, a free one. It keeps your routes in one place, so they reach your watch and stay yours.

Is Traceway free, with no subscription?

Yes, and it always will be. There are no ads, no subscription, and nothing to unlock, so every feature is open to everyone, even sending routes to your watch or bike computer. Traceway is made by one person who loves the sport. If you enjoy it, you can chip in for the hosting.
