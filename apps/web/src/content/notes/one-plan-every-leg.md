---
title: One plan, every leg
description: A brick, a triathlon, or a Sunday with friends who meet you at the handover.
published: 2026-10-12
---

Some days are more than one sport. A ride with a run straight after it. A swim in the lake, then the bike, then the run. Traceway plans those as one, with a leg for each sport and a transition where they meet.

Pick a race and the lengths fill in, from a super sprint to a full Ironman. Or set each leg yourself. The swim you draw on the water, because no routing engine can route there. Every leg on land comes from the map like any other loop.

When friends meet you with your gear, chain the legs. Put each handover where you will meet, and give each leg the length you want to cover on the way there. The line winds out to reach it, and it never turns back on a road you already used. Each leg leaves as its own course file, ready for its place in a multisport activity on the watch.
