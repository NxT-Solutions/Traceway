---
title: Onto the computer
description: The course file is what rides with you. The phone stays home.
published: 2026-10-03
---

A saved route becomes a GPX file and a Garmin FIT course. Import it in Garmin Connect, sync the bike computer, or copy the FIT into `Garmin/NewFiles` over USB.

The head unit already has its own map. Traceway does not send tiles. It sends the line, the elevation, and the turns. The course name on the device is the first 15 characters, so the short name is stored on its own.
