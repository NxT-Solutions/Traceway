---
title: Onto the computer
description: The course file goes with you. The phone stays home.
published: 2026-10-03
---

A saved route becomes a GPX file and a Garmin FIT course. Import it in Garmin Connect and sync a watch or a bike computer, or copy the FIT into `Garmin/NewFiles` over USB. A watch or GPS that reads GPX can follow the same line.

The device already has its own map. Traceway does not send tiles. It sends the line, the elevation, and the turns. The course name on a Garmin is the first 15 characters, so the short name is stored on its own.
