title: From the driveway
description: A loop starts at the pin you already know, and comes back there.
published: 2026-10-01
---

Pick a sport, a distance, and the ground you want under the tyres. Traceway draws a loop from that pin. The length is a target. A good line near 40 km beats a dull line that is exact.

Surface comes from OpenStreetMap. Where the map does not say paved or dirt, the line stays unknown. You can see that before you leave.
